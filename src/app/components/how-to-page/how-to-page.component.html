How to Play:

Every day, Gatekeepr picks a new artist.

Your Goal: Name songs by today’s artist.

Type a song title into the guess box.

Autocomplete will help, but you can type any title you think is correct.

You have 3 lives.

Incorrect guesses cost a life.

Tier up by getting songs right. 

The more correct songs you name, the higher your tier goes.

Tip: Try deep cuts! this game rewards real fans.
